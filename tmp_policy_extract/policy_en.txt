Section 1: Security & Privacy Policy 
 
Aoser places the highest priority on the security of user data and is committed to strictly protecting 
personal information in accordance with international standards. 
 
1. Scope of this Policy:  
This policy applies to the collection, use, disclosure, and protection of personal data from: 
 Service Users (Customers) 
 Service Providers (Freelancers) 
 
2. Data Collection and Use: 
 Only essential data will be collected, such as name, phone number, email, photos, location, and 
identity verification documents. 
 Data will be used solely for service-related purposes: identity verification, bookings, 
coordination, and invoicing. 
 
3. Data Protection: 
 Data encryption is applied to all transactions. 
 Information is stored on secure servers with backup systems in place. 
 Strict internal access controls are enforced. 
 Two-factor authentication (2FA) is required for critical accounts. 
 
4. Data Sharing and Disclosure: 
Aoser will not disclose user data to third parties unless: 
1. Explicit consent is given; 
2. Required by law or government agencies; 
3. Necessary for user safety and security. 
  
5. User Rights: 
 Users can request access, correction, or account deletion at any time. 
 Users have the right to file complaints regarding personal data breaches.
